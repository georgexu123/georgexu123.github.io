English

Where should I begin? Hundred Stops is a trading journey with a finish. Endless Trade lets you play at your own pace. Company mode is for monthly production, sales, cash management, reports, and investment. The player manual is available on the home screen.

Where is my progress? The three modes save separately on your device. Reopen the app and select the same mode to continue. The game does not keep a copy on a developer server; check your device backup settings before removing the app.

Something went wrong? Email georgexu123@163.com. Please include your device model, OS version, game mode, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot helps, but please send only what you are comfortable sharing.
