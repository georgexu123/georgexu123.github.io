需要帮助？

听野无需账户，大多数问题都可以通过重新打开应用、检查系统音量或恢复购买解决。

简体中文

快速帮助

  • 使用首页的大播放按钮开始聆听，上下滑动可切换声景。
  • 通过“分类”按自然环境浏览；列表播放按钮会直接播放声音。
  • 在“设置 > 播放”中启动或取消睡眠定时器。
  • 播放期间支持后台音频和锁屏控制。
  • 在“设置 > 声音来源与致谢”查看作者、来源和许可。

播放被中断

请重新打开 App，检查 iPhone 的系统媒体音量，再次点击播放。如果问题仍存在，请在邮件中附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、App 版本、所用语言和声音名称。

恢复购买

打开双轨解锁页面，点击底部的“恢复购买”。请确认设备登录了最初购买时使用的 Apple 账户。恢复购买不会重复收费。

通常在 3 个工作日内回复。

English

Quick help

  • Use the large Play button on Home to begin listening. Swipe vertically to switch soundscapes.
  • Use Categories to browse by natural environment; a row’s Play button starts the sound directly.
  • Open Settings > Playback to start or cancel the sleep timer.
  • Background audio and Lock Screen controls are available while a sound is playing.
  • Open Settings > Sound Sources & Credits for creator, source, and license information.

Playback was interrupted

Reopen the app, check your iPhone’s media volume, and press Play again. If the issue continues, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, selected language, and sound name in your email.

Restore a purchase

Open the dual-track unlock screen and select Restore Purchase at the bottom. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring does not charge you again.
