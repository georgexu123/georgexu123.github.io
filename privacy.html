开发者不收集的数据

当前版本不会把你的聆听记录、收藏、保存的组合、设置或设备标识发送给开发者或开发者运营的服务器。App 不包含开发者接入的广告、分析、跟踪、用户画像、数据经纪共享或第三方崩溃上报 SDK，也不会请求访问你的位置、麦克风、照片、通讯录或健康数据。

设备本地数据

收藏声音的标识、保存的双轨组合及其音量、最近播放的声音标识、播放音量、循环与睡眠定时器偏好，以及恢复播放所需的状态，只会通过 iOS 的 App 存储保存在你的设备上。这些信息仅用于提供你选择的功能，不会发送给开发者。

网站、内置内容与外部链接

音频和图片均随 App 打包，因此核心聆听不会连接开发者服务器。公开网站只会在浏览器本地存储中保存你选择的阅读语言，不包含开发者接入的广告或分析服务。网站托管于 GitHub Pages；GitHub 可能依照其隐私声明处理常规网页请求信息。

“声音来源与致谢”页面列出内置音频的来源和许可。只有在你主动点击时，才会打开来源或许可网站。这些外部网站可能收到 IP 地址、浏览器信息等常规连接信息，并适用各自的隐私政策。

支持邮件

当你选择“联系支持”时，App 会打开系统邮件应用。只有在你主动发送邮件后，开发者才会收到发件邮箱、主题、正文，以及你自行提供的设备信息或附件。这些信息仅用于回复和排查问题，不用于广告、用户画像或跟踪，并只在处理请求及相关记录所需的合理期限内保留。

保留、删除与选择

本地偏好会保存在你的设备上，直到你在 App 提供相应入口时修改或清除，或者删除 App。按照 iOS 的行为，删除 App 会移除其本地数据。你可以通过清除浏览器的网站数据来删除网站保存的语言选择。开发者没有你的账户资料或这些信息的服务器副本可供删除。App Store 购买记录由 Apple 管理，并可能依据 Apple 的保留规则继续用于恢复购买。

除你主动通过支持邮件提供的信息外，当前 App 不会向开发者传输个人数据。你可以就隐私问题、支持邮件删除请求或更正请求联系我们。

Scope and publisher

This policy applies to the Nature’s Hush iOS app and its public product, privacy, and support pages. The app is published by fang xu. Nature’s Hush works without an account.

Data the developer does not collect

The current version does not send your listening activity, favorites, saved mixes, settings, or device identifiers to the developer or a developer-operated server. It contains no developer-added advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, data-broker sharing, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does not request access to your location, microphone, photos, contacts, or health data.

Data stored on your device

Favorite sound identifiers, saved dual-track mixes and their volumes, recently played sound identifiers, playback volume, loop and sleep-timer preferences, and playback-resume state are stored locally using iOS app storage. They are used only to provide features you choose and are not transmitted to the developer.

In-app purchases

The one-time unlock and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The app requests product information and receives verified purchase-entitlement status so it can unlock paid features. The developer does not operate a purchase server and does not receive your complete payment-card details. Apple may process transaction, Apple Account, device, fraud-prevention, tax, and related information under Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

Website, bundled content, and external links

Audio and artwork are bundled with the app, so core listening does not contact a developer server. The public website stores only your selected reading language in your browser’s local storage and includes no developer-added advertising or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy statement.

The Sound Sources & Credits page lists source and license information for bundled audio. Source and license websites open only when you choose a link. Those external websites may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser details and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Support emails

When you choose Contact Support, the app opens your mail app. If you send a message, the developer receives the sender address, subject, message, and any details or attachments you choose to provide. This information is used only to reply and troubleshoot, is not used for advertising, profiling, or tracking, and is retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and related records.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local app preferences remain on your device until you change or clear them where the app provides that control, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data according to iOS behavior. You can remove the website’s saved language by clearing site data in your browser. The developer has no account profile or server copy of this information to delete. App Store purchase records are controlled by Apple and may remain available for purchase restoration under Apple’s retention rules.

Apart from information you voluntarily send in a support email, the current app does not transmit personal data to the developer. You may contact us with a privacy question, deletion request concerning support correspondence, or correction request.

Policy changes and contact

If a future version changes these practices, this policy, the app’s privacy manifest where applicable, and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before release.
